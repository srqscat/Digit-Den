    Make 24

    Use all four numbers once, with + − × ÷ and parentheses, to make 24.How to play

    Tap the cards and buttons to write your answer. Tap anywhere in the answer bar to move the cursor, and new pieces go in at the cursor. The answer is checked as soon as all four numbers are in and every parenthesis is closed. × and ÷ go before + and −, so use parentheses to change the order.

    Keyboard: 1–4 add a card, + - * / ( ) add a symbol, ← → move the cursor, Backspace deletes.
