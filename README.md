# Digit Den

Small math and logic puzzles you can play in your browser.

**Play it here:** https://srqscat.github.io/digit-den/

## Games

- **Crack the Code**: find a hidden 4-digit code. Digits can repeat.
  - Easy: every digit you guess is colored.
  - Medium: you can ask for clues.
  - Hard: you only see counts, not which digits they refer to.
  - Extreme: like Hard, but your guesses disappear, so you play from memory.
- **Make 24**: use four numbers once each, with + − × ÷ and parentheses, to make exactly 24. A stopwatch times you, and you can choose numbers from 1–9 or 1–13.

## Features

- Works on phones and computers, in light and dark mode
- English, Español, Français and 中文
- Playable with a keyboard or a screen reader
- Stats and streaks are saved in your browser
